# churn-analysis-stadioequities

STADIOEquities is currently facing a critical challenge with customer retention. According to the briefing pack, 41% of accounts never fund and 31% of accounts become dormant within six months. This recurring issue wastes marketing spend, reduce assets under adminstration and undermines the company's growth ambitions. A churn prediction project will add significant value by enabling STADIOEquities to identify accounts at risk of dormancy early in their lifecycle. With predictive insights, the company can design targeted interventions such as personalized onboarding support, tailored communication and retention offers. This directly addresses the company's current state of struggling to convert registrations into active investors and aligns with their strategic goal of improving long-term customer engagement. By applying data science methods to predict churn, STADIOEquities can reduce wasted acquisition costs, improve retention rates and strengthen the foundation for premium subscription growth. This project is therefore highly relevant to their business needs and offers a pathway to sustainable profitability.
